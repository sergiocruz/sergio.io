A short, beginner-level course for people who need to tell which kind of tool
fits a problem. Estimating a price and flagging fraud call for different things
than a chatbot does. This course shows you how to spot the difference.

## What You'll Learn

- Why machine learning trades certainty for probability
- What kind of answer a model actually gives you
- The problems it handles well
- Where the time and money go in an ML project
- How a model arrives at its number
- Splitting work between machine learning and generative AI
- What to bring to a specialist before starting
