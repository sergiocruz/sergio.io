A short course for engineers and technical leaders whose best arguments keep
losing in the room. It shows how to frame technical decisions in terms the
business already cares about.

## What You'll Learn

- Why a correct technical argument can still lose
- Turning technical state into business consequence
- Calibrating your message to the audience
- Presenting tradeoffs as decisions leaders can make
- Mapping your backlog to business strategy
- Making the case for non-feature work
- Handling pushback and negotiating scope and sequencing
